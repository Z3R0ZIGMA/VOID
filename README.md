# VOID

You are the last light in the dark. Your light fades every second. Gather motes to keep burning, and keep away from the shades drawn to your glow.

**Play:** open `index.html` in any modern browser. It's a single file with no dependencies.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Mouse | Touch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Move | WASD / arrows | move cursor | drag |
| Pulse (costs 12 light, banishes shades) | Space | click | double-tap or two-finger tap |
| Pause / Mute | P / M | | |

## How it works

- **Light** is your health and your vision. The less light you have, the less of the void you can see.
- **Motes** (gold) restore light. Grab them quickly one after another to build a chain multiplier.
- **Lanterns** show up after every 25 motes and fill your light completely.
- **Shades** (violet) drift toward you. **Stalkers** (pink) are faster and appear more often as time goes on. Only their eyes show in the dark.
- The game gets harder over time: your light drains faster, and shades spawn more often and move faster.

Your best score is saved in your browser.
